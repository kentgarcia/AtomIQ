## 📚 Lessons (3)

### Lesson 1: Meet the Atom
**ID**: `meet-the-atom`  
**Duration**: 6 minutes | **XP**: 50

**Learning Flow**:
1. **Briefing**: Introduction to atomic structure
2. **MythBuster**: "Atoms are the smallest things that exist" → Reality: Atoms contain protons, neutrons, and electrons
3. **Micro-Learn**: The Three Musketeers of the Atom (protons, neutrons, electrons)
4. **Lab**: Interactive - Build an atom and identify which particle determines the element
5. **Assessment**: Quiz on identifying elements by atomic number
6. **Debrief**: Atomic Master achievement

**Key Concepts**:
- Protons (positive, define element)
- Neutrons (neutral, add mass)
- Electrons (negative, orbit nucleus)
- Atomic number = protons

---

### Lesson 2: What is an Isotope?
**ID**: `what-is-isotope`  
**Duration**: 7 minutes | **XP**: 60  
**Prerequisites**: meet-the-atom

**Learning Flow**:
1. **Briefing**: Understanding atomic twins
2. **MythBuster**: "All atoms of the same element are identical" → Reality: Isotopes have different neutrons
3. **Micro-Learn**: Isotopes explained (same protons, different neutrons)
4. **Lab**: Interactive - Isotope Detective game
5. **Assessment**: Quiz on isotope stability
6. **Debrief**: Isotope Expert achievement

**Key Concepts**:
- Isotopes = same element, different neutrons
- Carbon-12 (stable) vs Carbon-14 (radioactive)
- Neutron-to-proton ratio determines stability
- Notation: Element-Mass (e.g., Carbon-14)

---

### Lesson 3: Natural vs. Artificial Radiation
**ID**: `background-radiation`  
**Duration**: 8 minutes | **XP**: 70  
**Prerequisites**: what-is-isotope

**Learning Flow**:
1. **Briefing**: Discover natural radiation
2. **MythBuster**: "All radiation is man-made" → Reality: 80% is natural (sun, rocks, bananas)
3. **Micro-Learn**: Sources of background radiation
4. **Lab**: Interactive - Radiation Source Detector (find highest exposure location)
5. **Assessment**: Quiz on radiation exposure percentages
6. **Debrief**: Radiation Reality Check achievement

**Key Concepts**:
- Natural sources: cosmic rays, radon, terrestrial, internal
- 80% natural, 20% artificial (mostly medical)
- Radon = largest natural source
- Altitude increases cosmic radiation exposure
