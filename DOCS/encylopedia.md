ENCYLOPEDIA OF RELATED TERMS AND PEOPLE TO NUCLEAR SCIENCE

A–Z Nuclear Science Terms 
A
Absorbed Dose – Radiation energy absorbed by matter, measured in gray (Gy).
Alpha Particle (α) – Heavy particle (2 protons + 2 neutrons) emitted during decay. Atom – Smallest unit of matter.
Atomic Number – Number of protons in an atom.
B
Background Radiation – Natural environmental radiation.
Becquerel (Bq) – Unit of radioactivity.
Beta Particle (β) – High-speed electron/positron from decay.
Binding Energy – Energy holding the nucleus together.
C
Chain Reaction – Self-sustaining series of fission events.
Control Rods – Rods that absorb neutrons to regulate reactors.
Critical Mass – Minimum fissile material needed for sustained fission.
Cyclotron – Particle accelerator producing isotopes.
D
Decay – Process where unstable nuclei emit radiation.
Dose Rate – Radiation absorbed per unit time.
Dosimeter – Personal radiation exposure detector.
E
Electron – Negatively charged atomic particle.
Enrichment – Increasing U-235 concentration.
Exposure – Contact with radiation.
F
Fission – Splitting heavy nuclei to release energy.
Fusion – Joining light nuclei to release energy.
Fuel Rods – Tubes containing fuel pellets.
G
Gamma Ray (γ) – Highly penetrating electromagnetic radiation.
Geiger Counter – Radiation-detection instrument.
Gray (Gy) – Unit of absorbed dose.
H
Half-Life – Time for half of a radioactive sample to decay.
Heavy Water – Deuterium-rich water used as moderator.
Hot Cell – Shielded facility for handling radioactive materials.
I
Ionizing Radiation – Radiation capable of removing electrons.
Isotope – Atoms with same protons, different neutrons.
Ion Chamber – Device measuring dose rates.
J
Joule (J) – Unit of energy.
K
K-Shell – Innermost electron shell.
Krypton-85 – Radioactive gas from fission.
L
Linear Accelerator (LINAC) – Machine for radiotherapy/particle acceleration.
Low-Level Waste (LLW) – Mildly radioactive waste.
Leak Test – Procedure ensuring sealed sources don’t release contamination.
M
Moderator – Slows neutrons (water/graphite).
Meltdown – Reactor accident where fuel overheats.
Mass Defect – Difference between mass of nucleus and sum of nucleons.
N
Neutron – Neutral nuclear particle.
Nuclear Reactor – Device controlling fission.
Nucleus – Central core of an atom.
O
Outage – Planned reactor shutdown.
Oxide Fuel – Common reactor fuel in pellet form.
P
Proton – Positively charged nuclear particle.
Photon – Light particle; gamma/X-ray are high-energy photons.
Plutonium-239 – Fissile isotope used in reactors/weapons.
Q
Quantum Energy – Discrete packet of energy.
Quark – Particle composing protons and neutrons.
R
Radiation – Energy emitted from atoms.
Radionuclide – Radioactive isotope.
Reactor Core – Region where fission happens.
Roentgen (R) – Older unit of exposure.
S
Sievert (Sv) – Unit of biological radiation dose.
Shielding – Barriers reducing radiation.
Scintillation Detector – Device detecting radiation through light flashes.
T
Thermal Neutron – Slow neutron ideal for fission.
Transmutation – Changing one element into another.
Tritium – Radioactive hydrogen isotope.
U
Uranium-235 – Fissile isotope used in reactors.
Uranium-238 – Common isotope, fertile.
Uranium Hexafluoride (UF₆) – Compound used for enrichment.
V
Void – Gas bubble in coolant affecting reactivity.
Vitrification – Turning nuclear waste into glass.
W
Waste (Radioactive) – Radioactive by-products requiring management.
Watt (W) – Unit of power.
X
X-ray – Electromagnetic radiation produced by electron interactions.
Y
Yellowcake – Concentrated uranium ore.
Yield – Energy released by a nuclear explosion.
Z
Z-Number – Atomic number (proton count).
Zero Power Reactor – Research reactor with minimal power.

A–Z Notable People in Nuclear Science
A
Carl David Anderson – Discovered positron (important in beta decay).
B
Henri Becquerel – Discovered natural radioactivity.
Niels Bohr – Developed atomic model; contributed to fission theory.
C
Marie Curie – Pioneer of radioactivity; discovered radium & polonium.
Pierre Curie – Partner in radioactivity research.
James Chadwick – Discovered the neutron.
F
Enrico Fermi – Built the first nuclear reactor; major contributor to reactor physics.

G
George Gamow – Explained alpha decay via quantum tunneling.
H
Otto Hahn – Discovered nuclear fission (Nobel Prize).
Werner Heisenberg – Quantum physicist; worked on German nuclear program.
Yukawa Hideki – Proposed the meson theory explaining nuclear forces.
L
Ernest O. Lawrence – Invented the cyclotron.
M
Lise Meitner – Co-discovered nuclear fission; provided theoretical explanation.
N
Yoshio Nishina – Father of Japanese nuclear physics.
O
J. Robert Oppenheimer – Scientific director of the Manhattan Project.
R
Ernest Rutherford – Father of nuclear physics; discovered the nucleus.
Wilhelm Roentgen – Discovered X-rays.
S
Andrei Sakharov – Soviet physicist; key figure in hydrogen bomb development; human-rights advocate.
Glenn T. Seaborg – Discovered plutonium and many transuranium elements
Frederick Soddy – Explained isotopes and radioactive decay chains.
Leo Szilard – Conceptualized chain reactions; advocated for nuclear control.
T
Edward Teller – Key figure in hydrogen bomb development.
Louis Turner – Early nuclear theorist.
V
John Von Neumann – Contributed to nuclear calculations and implosion design.
W
Eugene Wigner – Developed reactor physics; early Manhattan Project scientist.
Y
Chen-Ning Yang – Made contributions in particle physics related to nuclear theory.
